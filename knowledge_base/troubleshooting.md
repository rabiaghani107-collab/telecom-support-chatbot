# TelcoMax Troubleshooting Guide

## No Signal / No Service
1. Toggle Airplane Mode on and off.
2. Restart your device.
3. Remove and reinsert your SIM card.
4. Check the TelcoMax coverage map at telcomax.com/coverage.
5. If the issue persists, contact support to check for local outages.

## Slow Data Speeds
1. Check if you have exceeded your data limit — speeds are throttled after the cap.
2. Toggle mobile data off and on.
3. Switch between 4G/LTE and 5G in your device settings.
4. Reset network settings: Settings > General > Reset > Reset Network Settings.
5. If in a congested area, try connecting at a different time.

## Cannot Make Calls
1. Ensure your account is active and not suspended.
2. Check that you have sufficient call minutes remaining (prepaid).
3. Verify Do Not Disturb mode is off.
4. Try dialing *#31# to check call-barring status.
5. Contact support for SIM or network provisioning issues.

## Cannot Send SMS
1. Check your SMS limit under your current plan.
2. Delete old messages — full inbox can block incoming texts.
3. Ensure the message center number is correct: +1-555-000-0000.
4. Restart the messaging app or try the default SMS app.

## Voicemail Issues
1. Dial *86 to access voicemail.
2. If voicemail is not set up, dial *86 and follow prompts to create a PIN.
3. To reset voicemail PIN, contact support or visit My TelcoMax app.
4. Visual voicemail requires the TelcoMax app installed on your device.

## Hotspot / Tethering Not Working
1. Verify your plan includes hotspot — Basic plan does not include it.
2. Go to Settings > Cellular > Personal Hotspot and toggle on.
3. If using USB tethering, ensure drivers are installed.
4. Hotspot data is separate from regular data for Standard plan (5 GB limit).

## International Roaming
1. Enable international roaming in My TelcoMax app or call support.
2. Premium plan includes 100 international minutes; Unlimited includes 500.
3. Data roaming rates: $10/day for unlimited roaming data in 40+ countries.
4. To avoid surprise charges, enable Travel Pass before departing.

## Device Compatibility
- TelcoMax supports all GSM-compatible devices.
- 5G service requires a 5G-capable device and is available in select cities.
- To check compatibility, visit telcomax.com/byod or text BYOD to 611.
- eSIM is supported on iPhone XS and later, Samsung Galaxy S20 and later.
