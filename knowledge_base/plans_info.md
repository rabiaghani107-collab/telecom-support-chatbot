# TelcoMax Plans — Detailed Guide

## TelcoMax Basic — $29.99/month
- **Data:** 5 GB high-speed (4G LTE)
- **Calls:** 200 minutes nationwide
- **SMS:** 500 text messages
- **Features:** Voicemail, Caller ID
- **Hotspot:** Not included
- **International:** Not included
- **Best for:** Light users, secondary lines, seniors

## TelcoMax Standard — $49.99/month
- **Data:** 20 GB high-speed (4G LTE / 5G where available)
- **Calls:** 500 minutes nationwide
- **SMS:** 1,000 text messages
- **Features:** Voicemail, Caller ID, 5 GB Hotspot
- **International:** Not included (add-on available: $15/month)
- **Best for:** Average users, professionals

## TelcoMax Premium — $79.99/month
- **Data:** 50 GB high-speed (5G priority)
- **Calls:** 1,500 minutes nationwide
- **SMS:** 3,000 text messages
- **Features:** Voicemail, Caller ID, 20 GB Hotspot, 100 international minutes
- **International:** 100 minutes to 50+ countries
- **Best for:** Heavy users, frequent travelers

## TelcoMax Unlimited — $99.99/month
- **Data:** Truly unlimited (deprioritization after 50 GB)
- **Calls:** Unlimited nationwide
- **SMS:** Unlimited text messages
- **Features:** Everything included, 500 international minutes, Priority support
- **International:** 500 minutes to 80+ countries, $10/day roaming pass
- **Extras:** Free Apple TV+ or Disney+ subscription
- **Best for:** Power users, business professionals

## TelcoMax Prepaid Lite — $15.00/month
- **Data:** 2 GB high-speed
- **Calls:** 100 minutes
- **SMS:** 200 text messages
- **Features:** Pay-as-you-go, no contract, no credit check
- **Top-up:** Available in $10, $20, $50 increments
- **Best for:** Budget-conscious users, temporary lines

## TelcoMax Family — $119.99/month
- **Lines:** Up to 5 lines included
- **Data:** 100 GB shared high-speed
- **Calls:** 5,000 minutes shared
- **SMS:** Unlimited
- **Features:** Shared data pool, parental controls, Disney+ included
- **Additional lines:** $20/month per line (max 8 total)
- **Best for:** Families, households with multiple users

## Add-Ons Available for All Plans
- **Extra Data:** 1 GB = $10, 5 GB = $35, 10 GB = $60
- **International Calling Add-On:** $15/month (300 minutes to 50+ countries)
- **Device Protection:** $12/month (covers loss, theft, damage)
- **Cloud Storage:** 100 GB for $3/month
- **Premium Voicemail:** Transcription + email delivery for $5/month
