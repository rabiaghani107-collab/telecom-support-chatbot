# TelcoMax Policies & FAQs

## Billing Policy
- Bills are generated on the 1st of each month.
- Payment is due by the 15th. A $5 late fee applies after the due date.
- Accepted payment methods: credit/debit card, bank transfer, auto-pay, in-store cash.
- Auto-pay discount: $5/month off your bill when you enroll.
- Disputes must be filed within 60 days of the billing date.

## Cancellation & Refund Policy
- Postpaid plans: 30-day notice required. Early termination fee is $150 if within contract.
- Prepaid plans: No contract; unused balance is non-refundable.
- Device installment plans must be paid in full upon cancellation.
- Pro-rated refunds are available for mid-cycle cancellations of postpaid plans.

## Data Throttling Policy
- Once you exceed your plan's data limit, speeds are reduced to 2G (128 kbps).
- You can purchase additional data packs: 1 GB = $10, 5 GB = $35, 10 GB = $60.
- Unlimited plan users may experience deprioritization after 50 GB in congested areas.

## Account Security
- Two-factor authentication is available via the My TelcoMax app.
- Account PIN is required for all support interactions (4-digit PIN).
- SIM swap requests require in-store identity verification with photo ID.
- Unauthorized access attempts trigger automatic account lock after 3 failed attempts.

## Plan Changes
- Plan upgrades take effect immediately.
- Plan downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing cycle.
- One free plan change per billing cycle; additional changes incur a $10 fee.
- Family plan members must have account holder authorization for changes.

## Coverage Guarantee
- TelcoMax covers 98% of the US population with 4G LTE.
- 5G coverage is available in 200+ cities.
- If you experience persistent coverage issues at your home address, you may qualify for a free network extender device.
- 14-day coverage satisfaction guarantee for new customers.

## Device Trade-In Program
- Trade in eligible devices for bill credits up to $800.
- Device must be in working condition with no cracks or water damage.
- Trade-in values are determined at the time of submission and locked for 14 days.
- Credits are applied over 24 monthly installments.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I check my balance?
Dial *611 or open the My TelcoMax app.

### How do I activate a new SIM?
Visit telcomax.com/activate or call 1-800-TELCOMAX.

### Can I keep my phone number if I switch to TelcoMax?
Yes! We support number porting from all major carriers. The process takes 1-3 business days.

### What is Wi-Fi Calling?
Wi-Fi Calling lets you make calls over a Wi-Fi network when cellular signal is weak. Available on all plans at no extra charge.

### How do I set up auto-pay?
Log in to My TelcoMax > Billing > Payment Methods > Enable Auto-Pay.
